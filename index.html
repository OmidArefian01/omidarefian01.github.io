About Me

My work connects control theory, modeling, and intelligent decision-making for robotics and autonomous systems.

I am a Mechanical Engineering master’s student in Mechatronics and Robotics at Politecnico di Milano. My interests lie in robotics, control, autonomous systems, and the development of algorithms that move from theory to practical application.

Through academic and project work, I have developed experience in nonlinear control, path following, reinforcement learning, system identification, and experimental modal analysis.

I am particularly interested in building robotic systems that are not only high-performing, but also reliable, adaptive, and well-grounded in sound engineering principles.

Selected Projects

A snapshot of work in control, robotics, reinforcement learning, and system modeling.

Advanced Nonlinear Control

Optimal Obstacle-Avoiding Trajectory Design

Modeled a vehicle in a 2D environment and computed optimal trajectories using direct and indirect methods, then designed MPC and LQR controllers for the resulting control problem.

Robotics · MATLAB · ROS 2

Reinforcement Learning
